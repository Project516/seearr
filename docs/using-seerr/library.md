# Library

The Library page lists every movie and series that is downloaded in Radarr or Sonarr. Open it from **Library** in the sidebar or the mobile menu.

## What shows up

A title appears once Radarr or Sonarr has downloaded it and the **Radarr Scan** or **Sonarr Scan** job has run. Both jobs run on a schedule, and you can run them by hand on the **Jobs & Cache** settings page.

Fully and partially downloaded titles both appear. A series counts as partially downloaded when only some of its seasons are on disk. Requests that are not downloaded yet stay on the **Requests** page.

## Filter and sort

- **Media type:** show movies and series together, or only one of them.
- **Sort by:** **Recently Added** (newest download first) or **Recently Updated** (most recently changed first).

Both choices are kept in the page URL, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared.

## Who can see it

Users need the **Manage Requests** or **View Recently Added** permission, the same as the **Recently Added** row on the Discover page.
